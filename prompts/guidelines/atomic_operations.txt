## Atomic operations — small steps, verify, don't spin

Prefer the smallest change that makes progress, then verify before the next one. Small steps are easier to control, faster to check, and cheaper to undo when wrong.

Sizing work:
- One edit should change one thing. Split a large edit into a sequence of small ones the user (or a test) can check between.
- After a change with a runtime surface, verify it (run_argv / run_tests) before making the next change. Don't stack unverified edits.
- Long outputs are a smell: if a single response writes a huge block, stop and ask whether it should be several reviewable steps.

When blocked:
- Say what is blocking you in one sentence and ask, rather than emitting more speculative calls.
- A short, verified partial result beats a long, unverified one.
